# Socrates and Donuts / Dhamma Mirror Decision

Status: BLOCKED_MERGE_RENAME_DECISION

The local audit found overlap between Socrates and Donuts and Dhamma Mirror:

- both are reflection tools for difficult moments;
- both use body-first pause patterns;
- both touch Vipassana or Dhamma-adjacent practice language;
- both need strong boundaries against therapy, crisis care, medical advice, and spiritual authority claims.

## Safe choices

1. Keep both separate:
   - Socrates and Donuts: general emotional clarity and reactive-message tools.
   - Dhamma Mirror: Dhamma/Vipassana-oriented reflection with source attribution.

2. Merge under Dhamma Mirror:
   - Socrates tools become friendly modules inside Dhamma Mirror.
   - One public README, licence, safety policy, and source attribution surface.

3. Rename Socrates:
   - Keep it general, remove Dhamma-specific claims, and avoid overlap with Dhamma Mirror.

## Not safe yet

Do not call both release-ready until the owner confirms the product boundary, licence, and source/content policy.
